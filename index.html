The gear

Three routers. Ten sessions. One bag.

Every workshop uses one of these. Matched to skill level. All pocket-sized. All OpenWRT. All VPN-ready before you even unbox them.

"The Pocket Shield"
Beryl AX GL-MT3000

The one you'll actually carry every day. Pocket-sized, Wi-Fi 6, WireGuard at 300 Mbps. Plugs into any hotel or café and wraps every device in an encrypted tunnel — in under 60 seconds.

📡 One-tap VPN for all your devices
🛡️ Ad + tracker blocking via AdGuard Home
☕ Open WiFi survival kit

"The Road Warrior"
Slate AX GL-AXT1800

Built for the remote professional who can't afford a dropped call. Bond the café WiFi with your phone hotspot. If one dies, the other takes over automatically — mid-Zoom, no hiccup.

🔄 Multi-WAN failover + load balancing
🏠 Site-to-site tunnel to your home office
🔒 Kill switch + DNS leak protection

🐳 Docker · Pi-hole · Grafana

⚙️ Full OpenWRT · custom firmware
