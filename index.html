About the series

One hour. One skill. Every Friday over coffee.

Safe at the Table is a free workshop series for remote workers, freelancers, and anyone who uses a laptop in public. No tech background required. We meet at Afterglow every Friday and spend one hour on one skill that makes your everyday digital life meaningfully safer.

Most of us never think about this until something goes wrong. A card number stolen, an account taken over, a message sent in our name. These are not rare events. They happen on open WiFi, at tables like this one, to people who just wanted a good place to work.

We bring the tools and the know-how out of the IT world and onto the café table. Each session is hands-on, focused, and done before your lunch arrives. You will leave knowing something real.
